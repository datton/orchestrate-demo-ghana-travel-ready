Two quick checks

Is my passport valid long enough?

For a trip to Ghana. We use the stricter published rule: valid at least 6 months beyond the day you leave Ghana.

When should I get the yellow fever vaccine?

The certificate is required for arrivals aged 9 months and over. Vaccinate at least 10 days before you travel.
