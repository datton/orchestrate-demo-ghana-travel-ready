## C. IDEAS FOR GHANA (sites and areas): SAMPLE DATA ONLY

Source: Ghana Tourism Authority 2024 report via GhanaWeb; Ghana Ministry of Tourism site; read 20.11.2026.

### SAFE TO STATE
- Ghana welcomed 1,288,804 international visitors in 2024 (GTA 2024 Tourism Report).
- Kakum National Park (Central Region) is described by the government tourism site as Ghana's most visited attraction.
- Elmina Castle was seventh most visited in 2024 with 87,691 visitors (GTA report).
- Mole National Park (Northern Region): Ghana's largest protected area, 4,849 sq km; wildlife includes buffalo, roan antelope, elephants, warthogs, hyenas (government tourism site).
- Cape Coast Castle, Elmina Castle and Kakum are often visited together as a day trip from Accra or the coast.
- Accra areas named in official safety advice for burglaries: Airport Residential, Cantonments, Ridge, Kokrobite (FCDO). Crime is rising in Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi; avoid Nima and Agbogbloshie at night (Canada, 2 Nov 2026).

### CHECK (unconfirmed, do NOT show as fact)
- Entrance fees and opening hours for every site: sources conflict (e.g. Cape Coast Castle quoted at 5-10 cedis in one source and in dollars in another). Say "check with the site before you go".
- Hotel, guesthouse and flight prices and area ratings: no sourced figures. Budget tiers (Budget / Comfort / Premium) may suggest area types and trip styles, labelled **sample ideas, not live prices**. Show no price numbers, or show clearly labelled sample ranges in "sample data" boxes only.
- Tour-operator prices (a one-day castle and Kakum package was listed near US$80 by one operator): commercial figure, do not show.
- Do not invent hotel or airline names. Do not name a specific hotel.
